# Play Brick Battle

Open the arcade through **Display Items > Joystick**, then click the **third cabinet**, Brick Battle. **Close Game** returns to the lobby. Closing the arcade returns your pet to the desktop.

Choose **Easy**, **Normal**, or **Hard**. Higher difficulty makes your pet react faster, aim more accurately, and move its paddle faster. **Power ups** starts on, remembers your choice, and can be changed before a match.

Click **Start**. Round 1 begins with five grey dots on each side, both balls waiting on their paddles, and a **2:00** clock. Move your mouse up/down inside the field to move the left paddle. Click the field to launch. Your first click serves both balls together and starts the timer. A moving paddle angles its serve in that direction.

The right paddle belongs to your pet, which is centered directly over the bar and drawn in front of it. It tracks incoming balls and can launch replacement or sticky balls by itself after the first simultaneous serve. Balls bounce from both paddles, the top/bottom walls, and the center bricks. The center wall is three touching bricks wide with twelve rows spanning the field's height. A hit removes the brick. Paddle contact position and motion change the bounce angle; balls keep an even pace.

## Score and rounds

- A ball reaching the left open edge scores for your pet; one reaching the right edge scores for you, regardless of who last touched it.
- Your dots fill left to right; your pet's fill right to left. Each new red dot flashes for two seconds, then remains red.
- A side receives a pending replacement ball when a ball disappears and no live ball remains in that half. The ball follows its paddle; click to serve yours.
- Five red dots wins the round. At time-out, the most red dots wins. Equal dots make a draw, with no match point.
- The winning side gains one point on the split Score Card. It shows **You** and your pet's name, or **Vpet** if unnamed. The Round indicator counts played rounds, including draws.
- Each new round clears balls, powers, and dots, restores bricks and ordinary paddles, resets the clock, and waits for your simultaneous serve.
- Winning the first two rounds ends the match 2–0. Otherwise, play at least three rounds. After round three or any later round, the side leading on match points wins; equal scores require another round. A drawn round can finish a match if one side already leads.

**WINNER!** flashes yellow on the winning half. Your pet displays **Proud** if it wins, or **Sad** if you win. **Start** returns for another complete match. **Stop**, available even while waiting to serve, immediately clears play and prevents further scoring. Brick Battle does not change Dance Time or Simon Says high scores.

## Power ups

Every destroyed brick has a **25%** chance to drop a random orb. Orbs are **50% transparent** and move toward the paddle that last touched the brick-breaking ball. Catch one with a paddle to apply it. An uncaught orb disappears at the field edge. A new power replaces the previous power and returns an enlarged paddle to its base height, except for another yellow power.

| Orb | Paddle effect |
| --- | --- |
| Blue **x3** | The next ball hitting the paddle produces two copies, each ten degrees from the original. All three keep their speed, and the paddle returns to normal color. |
| Yellow **↕** | Increase height by 20% of base height, centered on the paddle. Further yellow orbs add 10% of base height each, bounded by the field. The effect lasts until replaced or the round ends. |
| Green **S** | The next ball sticks at its contact point and follows the paddle. Click to release yours; your pet releases its own. Other incoming balls still bounce. The paddle returns to normal color on release. |
| Red **!** | The next ball hitting the paddle becomes twice as large and blinks. A brick hit destroys that brick and every touching neighbor; the ball pulses and disappears. A paddle hit pulses and disappears, shaking and freezing that paddle for half a second. The charging paddle returns to normal color. |

The field uses the window's full width. Resizing adjusts its height and keeps existing brick damage, scores, balls, and paddle bounds. The header rearranges at narrow widths to keep the controls, score, and timer visible.
