Vpet
Your virtual desktop companion

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 version 1903 or later, or Windows 11,
with Microsoft .NET Framework 4.8 or later.

Open Vpet from the Start menu or optional desktop shortcut.
Right-click the pet or notification-area icon to open settings.
Left-click for Love and a brief shake. Drag to move your pet.
Choose Close Vpet from its menu to quit; closing Settings keeps it running.

The installer installs for your Windows account without administrator access.
Close Vpet before reinstalling, updating, or uninstalling it.
Uninstall through Windows Settings > Apps.

Settings and custom artwork are kept in:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\VpetPrototype
This existing folder name is retained for compatibility with earlier builds.
Updates and uninstalling preserve these personal files.

The pet works offline. When connected, it checks public releases at
https://github.com/JPMorgan93/vpet.
Settings > Advanced > Auto-update on app startup appears below the PC startup
option. Choose Yes to install the newest release automatically at app startup.
No is the default. Periodic checks while running announce available updates.
Check for Updates, below the last separator and above Close Vpet in the pet
menu, shows your current version and either
confirms it is up to date or offers an Update button for the available version.
Choose Update to begin. Progress is shown during the update.
Settings, artwork and shortcut choices are preserved. Release notes appear
only after the update succeeds and Vpet reopens, including all skipped releases
since the last version you ran. Up-to-date checks show status without old notes.
Users on version 1.0.0 must install 1.1.0 once to enable update checks.

FIND MY VPET
Settings > Advanced starts with the PC startup and auto-update options.
Below them, Find My Vpet defaults off. Enable it, choose ALT or CTRL, then
click the key box and press a key. Clicking clears the previous mapping;
the message confirms success or reports a failure. Defaults are ALT + ALT:
double-tap Alt within half a second. CTRL + CTRL also uses two taps.
Other bindings use the modifier and mapped key together.
Your shortcut circles the pet and dims the surrounding displays. The circle
follows the pet, even beneath application windows, and fades away over one
second. It keeps keyboard focus in your current app and passes clicks through.

SPRITE MAKER
Settings > Sprite > Open Sprite Maker lets you select animation frames from a
transparent PNG sheet. Set 1-10 frames per animation, then Tweak and Complete.
Each animation type has its own frame size, shared by all frames of that type.
Update Sprite Sheet replaces the PNG in an open project, keeping all mappings,
sizes, tweaks, and animation options. Keep the same sheet layout for mappings to
line up. Save Project to keep the revised image, then Tweak and Complete. Empty
or out-of-bounds selections are flagged for repair before exporting.
Drag the red border to move a selection or a corner to resize that animation.
In Tweak and Complete, artwork outside the frame is cut off in the preview
and final sprite. Projects retain the source art and your crop offsets.
Animation speed controls each selected type independently, from 0.25x to 3x.
The default is 1x. Moving its slider resumes playback immediately. Save Tweaks
or Complete saves these speeds in the project and exported pet. New project
saves and Sprite Maker exports require Vpet 1.11.7 or later. Older files keep
every mapping, size, offset, and speed, and gain empty frame slots 6 through 10.
The default pet uses the supplied Blue Dragon artwork with eight idle frames
and ten walking frames, playing idle and walking animations at twice their
previous speed. Updates retain your selected custom pet and its saved speeds; Restore
Default switches to Blue Dragon. Save default template exports a 320 x 360 PNG
with 32 x 36 cells. Use Sprite Maker to map that PNG before using it as a pet.
Both previews have zoom buttons, a percentage field, Fit, and 100% controls.
In Sprite Maker, drag the divider above Preview zoom to resize the display/edit
area inside the window. Upper controls scroll independently. A second divider
below the sheet resizes its validation area. Zoom and frame mappings stay intact.
Hold Ctrl and turn the mouse wheel to zoom around the pointer.
Magic Tweak aligns each pose's lowest visible pixels to a shared ground point.
Save .vpetproject files for editing; export .vpetsprite files for use in Vpet.
Load Last Project reopens the most recently opened or saved project, even after
restarting Vpet. Open or save a project once to enable it. Save changes before
closing to resume them later. Use Load Project to locate a moved file again.
Close Sprite Maker to preview your export, then choose Use this pet.
Use Sprite Maker to prepare PNG sheets; Upload Custom Sprite loads .vpetsprite.

MOVEMENT AND SETTINGS
Settings can be resized; text wraps and pages scroll vertically when needed.
Restricted movement uses a rectangular fence. Drag the plus control to move it
or the edges and corners to resize it. Hiding it keeps the boundary active.
Advanced > Sync Play Zone with Restricted Area is on by default: the pet uses
the toy play-zone fence for Restricted movement. Display restricted area and
Display Play Zone control the same fence, even when the chest is closed.
Turn sync off to restore the separate saved red restricted fence.
The fences are labeled Restricted Area and Play Zone above their top-left corners.
Random reactions occur Often: 15-30 seconds, Sometimes: 30-60 seconds,
or Rarely: 90-120 seconds, separately from spontaneous toy visits.

TOY CHEST
Right-click the pet > Display Items > Toy Chest to show a chest and fence.
Drag the middle control to move the play space; drag its edges or corners to
resize it. Drag the chest to place it inside. The zone fits one monitor's usable
area; move its center onto another monitor to move the play space there.
Right-click the chest > Display Play Zone to show or hide the fence. The fence
still keeps toys inside when hidden. Toggle Ball to add or remove the ball.
Clean Up Toys, directly below Display Play Zone, removes every toy and cancels
active play while leaving the chest and fence available.
It is greyed out when no toys are in the play space.
Click the ball for three bounces, each half as high. Left-drag to move it inside
the zone. Right-drag to aim, then release to launch along the red arrow.
The ball ricochets off the fence and slows to a stop.
Your pet walks to its resting point, pauses for a quarter second, shakes for
half a second, then returns it with random direction and strength without immediately chasing again.
Fetching temporarily overrides Static mode, zero speed and restricted roaming.
Your saved settings remain intact; a restricted pet walks back inside its fence.
Dragging the pet cancels play. Close Toy Chest in the chest menu puts away all toys.
Moving toys or the chest, opening their menus, and changing sound settings do not
pause the pet's motion. A pet approaching a moved toy keeps walking toward it.
An airborne coin keeps flipping while other toys are dragged.

Help Messages stays directly above Close Toy Chest at the bottom of the menu.
Toggle it for hints above the chest when hovering
over the fence border, its plus control, or a toy. Moving away hides the hint.

PLATE AND FOOD
Display Items is the first entry in the pet menu. Choose Plate to show or hide
a white plate. Left-drag it anywhere on the desktop, independently of fences.
It follows Window Location and remains available when the chest is closed.
The plate and pudding are 75% of their original size.
Right-click the plate > Pudding to select the default food and serve it.
Left-click an empty plate to serve the default again. Pudding has caramel,
whipped cream and a strawberry, with whole, two-thirds and one-third portions.
Food interrupts the pet's current action. It shows Hunger and walks above the
plate, behind the food, even in Static mode or outside movement fences.
Each bite has a one-eighth-second shake, then a one-eighth-second pause before
a third of the food disappears. Normal movement resumes after three bites.
Moving the plate redirects the pet; it waits until release before eating.
Picking up the pet or using another toy interrupts the meal. Right-click the
plate > Remove Plate to hide it, clear its food, and end feeding.
Visibility, position and default food are saved, but the plate
starts empty when Vpet restarts. Clean Up Toys does not remove the plate.
Hunger uses the Windows U+1F37D emoji, can be replaced under Personality, and
has an optional animation row in Sprite Maker alongside the other reactions.

NEW SPRITE AND TOY CONTROLS
Sprite Maker and Tweak and Complete both offer How to Guide with step-by-step
instructions. Sheet faces can be Left or Right. Optional Emote Animations adds
reaction rows; missing reactions keep their normal behavior. Download current
sprite sheet in Sprite settings exports your active pet's PNG for editing.

Toggle Triangle in the chest menu to add the instrument. Right-click the triangle
for Chime, Honk, or Drum. The saved sound applies to your taps
and the pet's replies. Each tap plays the selected sound. Pause
briefly after your last tap; your pet walks over and repeats the count and rhythm.
Drag the triangle to reposition it. A new tap during playback starts a new phrase.
Sound Setting, at the top of the triangle menu, opens a volume slider and Test
sound button. Test previews without saving or adding a remembered note. Save
applies the volume to both your taps and the pet's replies; Cancel discards it.

Coin, Card, and D20 are also toggles in the chest menu.
Click Coin: the pet stands beside it, pauses for a quarter second, shakes for half a second,
then flips its plain gold face upward around the horizontal X axis, even beyond
the fence or screen edge. After landing it
announces Heads or Tails. The coin itself has no face values.
Click a face-down Card: the pet stands beside it and announces a card. Choose
High (top half) or Low (bottom half) within 30 seconds, or the pet clears its
announcement and moves away. Settings and other movement pauses suspend the timer.
The flipped card excludes that exact announced card.
Correct guesses show Love; incorrect guesses show Sad; equal ranks show Question.
Ace (A) is lowest, followed by 2-10, J, Q, K. Suits do not affect rank. All 52 cards
reset each round. Click the revealed card only to flip it face down, then click
again to start another round. Drag Coin or Card to reposition it. Autonomous play
approaches first, turns a face-up card down, then starts and chooses High or Low.
The D20 uses its original size, with smaller numbers inside its central face
that stay clear of the lines. Left-drag to reposition it inside the zone;
right-drag and release to launch along its red arrow, or click for a random roll.
Hover over the D20 to stop the pet's current action, face the die, and show its
current number in the speech bubble. Moving away resumes the paused action.
It spins and ricochets inside the fence. The pet watches without chasing, then
announces a number from 1 to 20 when it stops. Game announcements are special
emotes, excluded from the default reaction/customization list.
When choosing the D20 for spontaneous play, the pet first walks beside the die,
then launches it, watches the roll, and announces the result. Your clicks and
pulls still launch it immediately while the pet watches from its current spot.

Pets also visit available toys on a randomized 60-120 second schedule. Toy visits
can temporarily leave Static or restricted movement, then restore normal rules.

A pause symbol appears only while Vpet settings are open. Toys and right-click
menus do not trigger it. It is
not a replaceable reaction. Try It Out previews temporarily replace this symbol.

JOYSTICK AND ARCADE
Display Items > Joystick adds the supplied Joystick.png artwork. Left-drag it
outside either fence; it follows Window Location. Click it to send the pet
behind it, even in Static mode or outside its restricted area. On arrival,
Arcade Window opens and the desktop pet hides. Closing Arcade returns the pet
behind the joystick and resumes normal rules. Right-click > Remove Joystick
puts it away. Closing the toy chest does not remove it.
Arcade Window always uses normal window stacking, allowing other applications
above it while keeping all Vpet desktop assets beneath it in every Window Location.
Outside the arcade, toys, the plate and food, and the joystick stay above the pet
sprite, including both portions during display crossings. The Toy Chest and
fences remain below it. All items continue to follow your Window Location setting.
Closing the arcade restores the assets' configured window stacking.

Click Dance Time, Simon Says, or Brick Battle in the first three arcade cabinets.
The other two are unavailable. Dance and Simon have Easy (default), Normal, and Hard, a three-second
countdown, WASD controls, and a circular-arrow button beside the top square to
switch to arrow keys. Close Game returns to the lobby. Click the score box to
see that game's high score for the selected difficulty. Scores, controls, and
music volume are saved across restarts.

Dance Time prepares all three supplied songs in the background. Start is ready
only when the selected song is fully decoded and queued in memory. Songs play
at their native sample rate; replay reuses the prepared song.
Dance Time uses the supplied song for each difficulty. Hit matching keys while
incoming targets overlap their squares: Good (1%) earns 10, Great (50%) earns
20, and Excellent (90%) earns 30. Three consecutive Excellents start a 1.0x
streak; further Excellents increase it by 0.1. Starting with the third Excellent,
points accumulate in a separate Streak total below the score box. When Good,
Great, a miss, or song completion ends the streak, only these points receive the
multiplier and join Calculated points. Earlier points and bonuses remain unchanged.
A miss banks the calculated total and resets the streak.
A bold, flashing Streak Combo indicator shows the multiplier at the top right.
Good or Great ends it and resets the multiplier to 1.0x after adding the bonus.
Successfully scored targets pulse briefly before disappearing.
An unmatched press or a target reaching the pet is a miss. Easy allows three
misses, Normal two, and Hard one; target speeds are 1x, 1.5x, and 2x.
The final miss ends the game and discards all its points. Finish the song to
bank remaining points and record a new high score if greater. The vertical
slider always controls music volume, including before starting, with its percentage
displayed below it. At 100%, music plays at the normal Windows/device-controlled
level. Lower settings progressively attenuate the music, with useful quiet levels
near the bottom; 0% is mute. Device master volume is unchanged. New preferences
default to 25%; saved levels remain. Volume, its slider, and percentage share
one padded neutral column. Song time is in the header to the left of Score Card.

Practice mode, to the right of Difficulty, is off by default and saved across sessions.
Turn it on between rounds to play the full song without scoring or a miss limit.
A numeric miss total replaces the miss-limit circles at the top left. Hits keep
their visual feedback; misses never end the song. High scores remain unchanged.

Background, below Difficulty, offers Dynamic (default), Static, or Off.
Dynamic rotates Dance Floor.png instantly by 90 degrees every one second.
Static keeps the original orientation. Off uses the dark neutral canvas color.
Simon Background, below Difficulty, offers On (default) for Simon Floor.png,
or Off for the same dark neutral color. Both choices are saved separately.
Both game spaces and floor backgrounds stay square when you resize the window.
Game names are in the window title. Pets, directional squares, labels, and targets
use the lobby's on-screen proportions as the window resizes. The key-mapping
button keeps its original size beside the upper square. Dance's larger bold
Misses label has a padded neutral block. Its fixed squares have neutral interiors
and thicker light outlines, with no dark outer band. Bright pink targets use
matching sizes and outline thickness. Visual overlap still matches scoring.

Simon starts with one button on Easy, three on Normal, and five on Hard.
Tones have smooth fades and rapid presses never cut off another tone.
Simon Says has no music. Watch the red/up, blue/down, green/left, and yellow/right
squares light up and play their unique tones, then repeat within five seconds.
Your inputs play the same directional tones. Each
correct round adds 50 pending points and one square to the sequence. Difficulty
changes the display speed: 0.9, 0.6, or 0.4 seconds per step. A wrong key or
timeout ends the game, banks your points, and records a new high score if greater.
The final correct input stays highlighted, then the pet looks down for a full
one-second pause before starting the next sequence.
Dance and Simon replace Start with Stop during countdowns and play. Stop cancels the
round, playback, and its score without changing high scores. Start returns after
stopping or finishing so you can replay.

BRICK BATTLE
The third cabinet opens a full-width paddle battle against your pet. Choose
Easy, Normal, or Hard; higher difficulty improves the pet's movement and aim.
Power ups defaults On and remembers your choice. Set match options before Start.
Move the mouse up/down in the field to move your left paddle. Click to serve
a waiting ball. Every round waits at 2:00 until you click; both initial balls
launch together. A moving paddle angles its serve. Your pet can serve its own
replacement balls afterward. Balls bounce off paddles, top/bottom walls, and
the three-column, twelve-row brick wall. Bricks disappear on impact.
A ball escaping either open side scores a dot for the opposite side. Your dots
fill left to right; your pet's fill right to left. New dots flash for two seconds.
Five dots, or the most when two minutes expire, wins a match point. A tie awards
none. Each new round resets the field, dots, powers, and clock and waits for you.
Score Card shows You and the pet's saved name, or Vpet. Two straight wins end a
match at round two; otherwise play at least three rounds, continuing if tied.
WINNER! flashes yellow. The pet shows Proud for winning or Sad for losing.
Start becomes Stop during pending/play; Stop ends play without further scoring.
Start afterward begins a new match. Other arcade high scores remain unchanged.

Each broken brick has a 25% power-up drop chance. Half-transparent orbs drift
toward the last touching paddle. Catch one; uncaught orbs disappear at an edge.
Blue x3 splits the next paddle hit into three balls ten degrees apart.
Yellow vertical arrows add 20% base paddle height; another yellow adds 10%.
Green S sticks the next ball at its contact point. Click to release it; the
pet releases its own. Later incoming balls bounce normally.
Red ! charges a double-size blinking ball. It destroys a brick and its touching
neighbors, or shakes/freezes a struck paddle for half a second, then pulses away.
A new power replaces the previous one. Empty halves receive a pending serve
after a ball disappears. Resizing keeps ongoing play and scores intact.

REMINDERS
Right-click the pet > Reminders, between Settings and Check for Updates with
a separator on each side, opens Reminder Window.
Add Reminder opens an Edit Reminder box above the saved, read-only list.
Use Edit on the left of a saved entry to change it.
Choose One-Time for a date and time, or Recurring for days from Sun through Sat.
Each selected day has its own frequency: Every, Every other, First, Second,
Third, or Fourth weekday of the month. Every is the default. Every other uses
the Sunday-Saturday week when that day was selected as its first week.
All selected days share one time, with explicit AM/PM controls, in PC local time.
Messages allow up to 200 characters, including clickable HTTP/HTTPS links.
Active is checked by default. Save stores the entry, Delete erases it, and
Cancel discards unsaved edits.
When due, the pet plays a simple three-tone chime and displays a separate
reminder bubble with Dismiss.
The bubble stays beside the pet and follows walking, dragging, and movement
between displays, updating alongside the emote speech bubble.
Hover over it to hold the pet still while reading or clicking. Dismiss closes
that occurrence; recurring reminders remain scheduled. One-time reminders
become Completed and do not repeat. Notifications do not steal keyboard focus.
Vpet must be running. After sleep or reopening, it shows each reminder's latest
missed occurrence. Pending messages survive restart and queue one at a time.
Saving edits restarts the schedule and clears the old pending message.
Reminders are kept in reminders.json beside settings.json; saves retain a .bak.
