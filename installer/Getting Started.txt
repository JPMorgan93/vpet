Vpet
Your virtual desktop companion

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 version 1903 or later, or Windows 11,
with Microsoft .NET Framework 4.8 or later.

Open Vpet from the Start menu or optional desktop shortcut.
Right-click the pet or notification-area icon to open settings.
Left-click for Love and a brief shake. Drag to move your pet.
Choose Close Vpet from its menu to quit; closing Settings keeps it running.

The installer installs for your Windows account without administrator access.
Close Vpet before reinstalling, updating, or uninstalling it.
Uninstall through Windows Settings > Apps.

Settings and custom artwork are kept in:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\VpetPrototype
This existing folder name is retained for compatibility with earlier builds.
Updates and uninstalling preserve these personal files.

The pet works offline. When connected, it checks public releases at
https://github.com/JPMorgan93/vpet.
Settings > Sprite > Auto-update on app startup appears below the PC startup
option. Choose Yes to install the newest release automatically at app startup.
No is the default. Periodic checks while running announce available updates.
Check for updates remains in the pet menu and installs available releases
directly, without a continue prompt. Progress is shown during the update.
Settings, artwork and shortcut choices are preserved. Release notes appear
only after the update succeeds and Vpet reopens. Up-to-date checks show status.
Users on version 1.0.0 must install 1.1.0 once to enable update checks.

SPRITE MAKER
Settings > Sprite > Open Sprite Maker lets you select animation frames from a
transparent PNG sheet. Set 1-5 frames per animation, then Tweak and Complete.
Each animation type has its own frame size, shared by all frames of that type.
Drag the red border to move a selection or a corner to resize that animation.
In Tweak and Complete, artwork outside the frame is cut off in the preview
and final sprite. Projects retain the source art and your crop offsets.
Both previews have zoom buttons, a percentage field, Fit, and 100% controls.
Hold Ctrl and turn the mouse wheel to zoom around the pointer.
Magic Tweak aligns each pose's lowest visible pixels to a shared ground point.
Save .vpetproject files for editing; export .vpetsprite files for use in Vpet.
Close Sprite Maker to preview your export, then choose Use this pet.
Existing five-column, ten-row PNG sheets are still supported.

TOY CHEST
Right-click the pet > Display Toy Chest to show a chest and rectangular fence.
Drag the middle control to move the play space; drag its edges or corners to
resize it. Drag the chest to place it inside. The zone fits one monitor's usable
area; move its center onto another monitor to move the play space there.
Right-click the chest > Display Play Zone to show or hide the fence. The fence
still keeps toys inside when hidden. Toggle Ball to add or remove the ball.
Click the ball for three bounces, each half as high. Pull back and release along the
red arrow. The ball ricochets off the fence and slows to a stop.
Your pet walks to its resting point, pauses for a quarter second, shakes for
half a second, then returns it with random direction and strength without chasing again.
Fetching temporarily overrides Static mode, zero speed and restricted roaming.
Your saved settings remain intact; a restricted pet walks back into its circle.
Dragging the pet cancels fetching. Turning off the chest also puts the ball away.

Toggle Help Messages in the chest menu for hints above the chest when hovering
over the fence border, its plus control, or the ball. Moving away hides the hint.
